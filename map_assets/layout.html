About the data

Equal-weight regions, NUTS 2024 geography.

Unemployment: annual EU-LFS rate, ages 15–74. GDP: economic activity per inhabitant in PPS, not residents' income. PPS uses national price adjustments, not separate regional adjustments.

Classes are based on quintiles of available regional values. Ties can produce unequal class sizes. Grey indicates an unavailable value; pale background areas are outside the study scope.

Flags: u low reliability; b time-series break; d different definition; p provisional; e estimated. No attached flag is not a quality guarantee.

The map is descriptive. It does not establish causation or individual-level relationships.

Sources: Eurostat unemployment, regional GDP and GISCO boundaries.
